##  Features

-  Fully self-contained **single-file** implementation (`doatas.py`)
-  Works **out-of-the-box** with a built-in synthetic NTN dataset
-  Supports **custom CSV input** via `DATA_PATH`
-  Compares **DO-ATAS vs. baseline ATAS** side by side
-  Prints **all 11 evaluation metrics** from the paper outline
-  Auto-generates a **4-panel visualization** of results
-  Deterministic runs via fixed `SEED = 42`
-  Zero external dependencies beyond `numpy`, `pandas`, `matplotlib`

---

##  Evaluation Metrics

The framework reports every metric described in the research outline:

| # | Metric | Unit | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Handover Delay (avg / total) | ms | ↓ lower is better |
| 2 | Throughput | Mbps | ↑ higher is better |
| 3 | Packet Loss Ratio | % | ↓ lower is better |
| 4 | RSS (Received Signal Strength) | dBm | ↑ higher is better |
| 5 | SNR (Signal-to-Noise Ratio) | dB | ↑ higher is better |
| 6 | Handover Success Rate | % | ↑ higher is better |
| 7 | Ping-Pong Rate | % | ↓ lower is better |
| 8 | Latency | ms | ↓ lower is better |
| 9 | QoS Score | [0–1] | ↑ higher is better |
| 10 | Network Stability | [0–1] | ↑ higher is better |
| 11 | Communication Reliability | [0–1] | ↑ higher is better |
